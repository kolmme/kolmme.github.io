CASE STUDY · PERSONAL PROJECT

Orbyte.

Orbyte AI is a personal AI assistant that lives on my own laptop. I say “Orbyte”, she listens, and she helps me organise my week, study maths and German, and control my PC — in Spanish, by voice.

RoleSolo project — idea, design and code
TimelineSeptember 2026 — today
StackPython · Flask · JavaScript · Claude API · Whisper · Git
Runs onMy Windows laptop, plus my phone through a private network

Why I built it

I moved to Berlin to study Software Engineering, learn German, and take a maths course. My week was spread across calendars, notes, chats, and apps.

I wanted one assistant that knows my schedule and my courses, that I can simply talk to, and that I can finally build and understand myself, piece by piece.

How it works

01Wake wordMy AI listens for “Orbyte” and can be set to react only to my voice.
02Speech to textWhisper runs on my laptop's graphics card and turns my voice into text in about half a second.
03ThinkSimple commands are answered instantly for free. Everything else goes to Claude, which can use real tools: calendar, Gmail, Spotify, timers and my notes.
04SpeakShe answers with a natural voice, sentence by sentence, so she starts talking before the full answer is ready. I can interrupt her anytime.

What she can do

  • Read and organise my Google Calendar and Gmail
  • Follow my maths course week by week and check every calculation with an exact calculator
  • Practise German with me: word of the day, flashcards and conversation
  • Play music, open apps, set timers and control my PC by voice
  • Search my notes and documents when I ask about a specific topic
  • Talk to me on Telegram when I'm away from my laptop

What I learned

  • Designing for voice is different: answers must be short, and the assistant must know when to stay quiet.
  • AI products need tests too. Writing hundreds of example phrases caught bugs before they reached me.
  • Building it with an AI coding assistant (Claude Code) taught me to plan, review, and test code instead of only writing it.
  • Costs matter: free local answers first, and the AI only when it is really needed.
